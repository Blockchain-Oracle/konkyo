<Steps>
<Step>

### Walk in

Open [konkyo.xyz](https://konkyo.xyz) and choose **step into the library**. The enter card asks for
**your name** and lets you **pick a look**; **enter →** and you're in as a guest. Nothing else is
needed.

</Step>
<Step>

### Let a starter fill a space, or bring your own

Home asks **what are you here for?** Pick a template, or **see it work on open material first**:

| Starter | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Study a sample course** | 5 open sources; the quiz starts once they're read |
| **Research a question** | A real deep search, which takes a few minutes |
| **Start an essay** | 3 papers and a note to write in |

Or **skip. I'll add my own files.** and drop files anywhere.

</Step>
<Step>

### Ask, practise, write

Ask from the box on Home or a space's dock. Every citation is ticked only once its quote is found
in the source; click it to open the passage. Then [practise](/docs/guide/practise) or
[write](/docs/guide/write) from the same sources.

</Step>
<Step>

### Keep it

**keep this on my other devices** (in the rail's settings card, or whenever a feature for kept
accounts asks) turns the guest into an account with Google, or with a six-digit code by email. Everything comes with you: spaces, sources, memory and practice.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Guests">
A guest lives in one browser. Signing a guest out deletes it and its library, so keep it first.
Guests get smaller daily allowances (60 answers, 3 deep searches, 40 files); a kept account gets
300, 20 and 300. See [Your account](/docs/guide/account).
</Callout>
