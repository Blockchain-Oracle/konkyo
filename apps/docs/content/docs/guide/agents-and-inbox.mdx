## Three kinds of agent

Make one from **Agents** (**New agent**), a space's **+ add** (**watch for new papers**), or a
space template. Each runs every day or every week at an hour you choose, in your time zone, once
right away, and then on schedule. Pause, edit or **run now** any time; **run history** keeps the
last ten.

| Agent | Each run |
|---|---|
| **Watch for new papers** | Searches OpenAlex for new open-access papers on your topic through a Zetaris table, skipping papers you already have. The newest (3 on the first run, up to 5 after) land in your Inbox, and each with an abstract is placed against your sources: **agrees with** or **disagrees with** one of your passages (quoted), or **new to your library** |
| **Quiz me on a schedule** | Fresh practice from a folder, checked against your sources |
| **Brief me on what changed** | A new audio brief of a space, only when sources were added since the last one |

A run with nothing new leaves one quiet slip instead of a pile.

| Tag | What it is | Button |
|---|---|---|
| **found** | A paper a watch found, placed against your sources | **import →** |
| **brief** | An audio brief is ready | **listen →** |
| **practice** | A scheduled quiz is ready | **practise →** |
| **review** | Your daily review reminder | **start review →** |
| **mail** | Mail to a space from someone outside it | **add to the space →** / **discard it** |
| **agent** / **notice** | A quiet run, or something that needs you (a connector to reconnect) | **open →** |

With notifications on (**Settings → notifications**), they arrive as push notifications on this
device too (one per watch run, not one per paper), and a review reminder comes at your reminder hour (18:00 by default) when something's
due. A kept account can turn on a **weekly digest by email** on Fridays.

## Catch me up

<Shot
  src="/shots/wide/catch-up.webp"
  alt="The catch me up panel open over Home, listing what moved: practice due, a file that needs you, 3 papers the watches found, a deep search's answer, 7 sources that landed in a space."
  caption="✦ catch me up: what moved since you last looked."
/>

**✦ catch me up** in the dock lists what waits on you and what moved since you last closed it
(the first time, the last 7 days): practice due, files that need you, deep searches to pick from,
watch finds, sources that landed, answers that finished. It's built from what happened, never
written by a model.
