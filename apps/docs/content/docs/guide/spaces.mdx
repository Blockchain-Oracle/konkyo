## Spaces

A space holds one subject or project. Its sources, the answers you pinned, its folders and an
audio brief sit on a wall in the space's own colour, and a **live round** plays its practice from
the space's header. Make one from **+** in the rail, from a
template (exam prep, essay or IA, literature review, reading group) or empty; a template adds the
pieces that kind of work needs, such as a watch for new papers or a weekly quiz. **this space knows
↗** opens [what the space knows](/docs/guide/knows); **wall** and **list** switch how its sources
are laid out.

## Sharing a space

The **invite** button on a space opens who's in it and its link.

- **Roles.** People who *can add* bring their own sources into the space; people who *can read*
  read everything, ask, pin and leave takes. The owner changes roles or takes people out, and a
  person taken out takes their sources with them. A member's sources stay theirs: leaving, they
  choose whether to take them out of the space.
- **The link.** One link at a time, giving the role it was made with. Choosing a role makes a new
  link and the old one stops working. Anyone who opens it walks in through the enter card, no
  account needed.
- **By email.** Type an address and Konkyo sends the link (a kept account can send 10 a day).
- **Together, live.** Who's here right now, sources landing as they're added, the space chat
  (mention **@konkyo** to have it answer from the space's sources, with citations), pinned
  answers and takes on a source, for everyone at once.
- **A live round.** Play the space's practice together: a six-character join code, eight
  questions, answers face down until everyone has answered or 30 seconds pass, then the bars, the
  faces and the passage. The recap offers the questions the group missed.

## Email a space

Each space has an address, shown in its invite popover to the owner and to people who add:
`<code>@in.konkyo.xyz`. Forward it a paper and it lands on the wall.

- **Who goes straight in.** Mail from the owner, or from someone who can add, whose own domain
  vouched for it (DMARC, or a DKIM signature of the sender's domain). The address alone never
  counts: anyone can type any From.
- **Everyone else waits.** Mail from anyone else lands in the owner's Inbox: *add to the space*
  or *discard it*. Nobody answering means it goes after 7 days.
- **What becomes a source.** Each attachment Konkyo reads (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Markdown, text,
  audio, video), up to 10 an email and within the day's file allowance; and the email itself when
  it says enough on its own (40 words or more once the quoted thread and signature are cut).
- **A new address.** The owner can make a new one from the same popover; the old one stops
  working at once.

## Signing in by email

Walk in as a guest, then keep it with Google or a six-digit code by email (it works for 10
minutes and 5 tries). Keeping it moves everything the guest had (spaces, sources, memory,
practice) into the account. **Sign in** on another device asks for the email you kept it with.

## Public spaces and articles

- A **public link** shows a space read-only to anyone, with the owner's own sources in it, and
  answers their questions from those sources with the same checks. Downloads are off unless the
  owner allows them.
- **Publish** turns a note into an article at `konkyo.xyz/a/<link>`. It needs a kept account
  with a handle, and a current Check my draft of the saved text; the article shows what the check
  found (how many claims held, partly held, weren't found or were contradicted). See
  [Write and publish](/docs/guide/write#publish).

## The weekly digest

Turn it on in Settings → Notifications (kept accounts): on Fridays, at your reminder hour, an
email lists what's new in your spaces, practice waiting and papers your watches found. A week
with nothing new sends nothing.
