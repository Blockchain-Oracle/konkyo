**What Konkyo knows** (from the rail's settings card, or **this space knows ↗** on a space) shows
everything Konkyo keeps about you or a space, assembled from what it stored; no model writes the
page when you open it. Your corrections are yours alone; a space's claims come from everyone's
comparisons in it.

| Section | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **coming up** | Practice due today, and when each watch looks again |
| **what it has made** | Practice sets, audio briefs and deep searches from the last 30 days |
| **how you like answers** | Your corrections, from a **not right** with a reason, a wrong passage, a practice question you marked wrong, or something you told it. The Planner reads the relevant ones before every answer |
| **words your sources use a lot** | Words that appear in two or more of your sources |
| **where your sources stand** | Claims from Compare answers (yours, and in a space everyone's), where the sources agree or differ, each stance from a passage you can open |
| **what it held back** | This week's answers: quotes found word for word, sentences rewritten, sentences dropped |

## Tell it, or make it forget

Type into **tell it something, like "cite page numbers"** and it's remembered. **cross out** any of
your corrections and Konkyo forgets it; **put back** tells it again.

Your corrections live in Meterless's H-MEM, and who said what in its World Model.
