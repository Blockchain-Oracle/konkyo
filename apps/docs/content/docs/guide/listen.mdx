## Audio briefs

**listen to this space** on a space's wall (also **an audio brief** in the add tray, a folder's
**Make an audio brief**, the orb, or a brief agent) makes a short conversation between two voices,
Bella and George, about what the space's sources say.

The script is written from the space's most central passages, and every factual line quotes one.
Before anything is recorded, each quote is checked in Zetaris and each line by the Verifier; a line
that doesn't hold is left out. Then ElevenLabs records the dialogue in one request and the
transcript is timed word by word, so each line's citation is there as you hear it. It lands in your
Inbox too. Play it at 0.75× to 2×, skip 10 seconds, or download the mp3.

## Talk to Konkyo

Tap the orb in the dock and say what you want. Your words appear as you say them, and the orb
works out what you meant:

| Say something like | It does |
|---|---|
| "Do these papers agree about naps?" | Answers from the space, checked, and reads the short answer back |
| "Find sources on slow-wave sleep" | A deep search into the space, importing what it shortlists |
| "Quiz me on this" | Makes practice and opens it |
| "Make a note…" | A new note |
| "Brief me" | An audio brief of the space |
| "New space for…" | An empty space |
| "Catch me up" | What moved since you last looked |
| "Remember that I want page numbers" | Saves it to what Konkyo knows |
| "Show me where it says that" | Opens the last answer's first citation |
| "That's all" | Closes the mic |

Two asks chain in one sentence ("find sources on spindles, then quiz me on what it finds").

## Read aloud

**▶ listen** on any answer reads it in your chosen voice (Bella, Alice, River, Daniel, Matilda or
Eric), the spoken sentence underlined. Choose the voice, and whether answers are read after a
spoken ask, in the dock's **♪** menu or **Settings → sounds & voice**.
