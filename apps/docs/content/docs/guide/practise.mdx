## Make practice

**quiz me** makes a set from the whole library, a space, or any files and folders (also from a
file's or folder's menu, a space's **practise this space**, or by saying it to the orb). Practice
is made from your sources, never your notes.

A set is written as it's checked: the **Tutor** drafts each question from a passage, the
**Verifier** checks its answer key, and **Zetaris** confirms its quote is in the source. Only
questions that pass all three are kept, and your round starts once six have passed. Corrections
you've given before are read first. [The same check, everywhere](/docs/agents/one-check-everywhere)

| You see | What happens |
|---|---|
| **not quite** | *Not quite. Try once more.* with **a hint**, then **another hint**, then **show me the answer** |
| **right ✓** | *That's it.*, and **why?** opens the passage it came from |
| **see it again** | **again**, **hard**, **good**, **easy**, each with when you'll see it next (keys 1-4). Konkyo preselects one from how you did |
| **•••** on a question | **Edit** (checked again; if the source doesn't back it you can still **Save anyway, unchecked**), **This is wrong** (re-checked with your reason; if the Verifier agrees, the question goes and Konkyo remembers), **Remove** |

Questions come back on a spaced-repetition schedule (FSRS, aiming at 90% recall, up to a year
apart). **today's review** gathers what's due plus today's new questions (15 a day by default, up
to 20 a round).

## Five games, the same checked questions

| Game | You |
|---|---|
| **recall sprint** | Say it in a few words, or fill the blank |
| **fix the false statement** | Spot what's false, then make it true |
| **find the evidence** | Pick the passage that says it, out of four from your own sources |
| **order the steps** | Put a real sequence back in order |
| **compare the sources** | Say whether two sources agree, then point to where each says so |

A game round is 8 questions, mixed across topics. **say it** answers out loud.

## Streaks

A day counts once you've really recalled something. You hold two **freezes** that cover a missed
day, and one comes back every 7 days you study. Missed two days? Two rounds today repair it.

## A live round, with friends

Start one from **Study** or a space's **▶ live round**. Everyone joins with a six-character code,
up to 30 people, and answers the same 8 questions face down until all have answered or 30 seconds
pass. Then the bars, the faces and the passage. The score is the team's, never a leaderboard, and
the recap offers the questions the group missed.

## From a file

- **Make notes** turns a file into cited notes, every quote checked.
- Select text in a PDF and choose **Flashcard**: it's checked against the source before it's saved.
