**Every answer shows its grounds.** Konkyo is one library for everything you study: papers,
lectures, notes, slides and recordings, in spaces you can share. Ask it anything, practise from it,
write with it, or listen to it. Behind every answer, a team of research agents works across your
sources: they answer only from them, check every quote word for word in Zetaris, remember your
corrections with Meterless engines, and show where your sources disagree. Anything they can't back
is rewritten; what still fails is dropped. *Konkyo* (根拠) means "grounds" or "evidence".

## Why it checks

Citations make an answer look trustworthy whether or not anyone opens them: in one study, only 23
of 319 knowledge workers checked the sources an AI gave them
([Lee et al., CHI 2025](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713778)). So Konkyo opens every citation
for you before it ticks it, and one click on a citation opens the source at the passage,
highlighted.
