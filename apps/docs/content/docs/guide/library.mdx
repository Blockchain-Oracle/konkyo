## Every way in

Open **+ add** on a space or the library, or press ⌘K anywhere.

| In the add tray | What becomes a source |
|---|---|
| **upload files** | PDFs, Word, slides, Markdown, text, recordings (`.pdf .docx .pptx .md .txt .mp3 .m4a .wav .weba .mp4 .mov .webm`), up to 2 GiB each; uploads resume after a reload |
| **upload a folder** | Everything inside it; at the top of your library its folders are kept |
| **a link or DOI** | A paper, an article, any web page. An arXiv or journal page reads the paper's PDF; a DOI pasted into ⌘K finds an open copy |
| **a YouTube video** | Its transcript, timed to the video: captions when it has them, otherwise transcribed |
| **record a lecture** | Recorded in the browser, then transcribed with who spoke when, so every answer can play the moment it cites |
| **email this space** | Mail forwarded to the space's own address: attachments, and the email itself when it says enough ([how](/docs/guide/spaces#email-a-space)) |
| **from Gmail / from Drive / from Notion** | An email and its attachments, a Drive file, a Notion page; Drive and Notion are re-read when they change |
| **find sources** | A deep search for open papers and web pages ([how](/docs/agents/deep-search)) |
| **a new note** | A note you write. Answers can cite it (checked against its own text); practice and Check my draft read only your sources |

You can watch each stage in the library: `Reading page n of N`, `Converting to PDF`,
`Transcribing`, `Indexing N passages`, `Checking into Zetaris`. Scanned pages are read with OCR and
marked **Text from scan**.

## When a file needs you

| Status | Why | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Locked PDF** | It's password-protected | **Upload an unlocked copy** |
| **Couldn't reach** | The link or video didn't answer, or a DOI has no free copy online | **Try again**, **Upload the PDF**, **Upload the video file instead** |
| **No readable text** | Nothing could be read, even with OCR | **Upload a copy with text** |
| **Duplicate** | The same file is already in your library | **Keep the existing one** |
| **Unsupported** | A kind Konkyo can't read | **Remove** |

## Around the library

- **Folder menu** (`•••`): **Chat with this folder**, **Find sources on this topic**, **Quiz me on
  this folder**, **Make an audio brief**, **Share…**, **Download as .zip**.
- **File menu**: **Open**, **Chat with this file**, **Quiz me**, **Make notes**, **Details** (a
  model-written summary, the citation you can edit, **Copy BibTeX**), **Move to…**.
- **Select several** (Shift-click for a range) to move them, download them, or **Export
  bibliography (.bib)**.
- **Trash** keeps what you delete for 30 days.
