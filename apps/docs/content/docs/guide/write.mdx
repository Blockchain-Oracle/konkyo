## Write with your sources beside you

A note has a heading, bold, italic, lists and quotes, saves as you type, and keeps a version every
ten minutes (name one any time, and restore it from **Versions**). The side panel has four tabs:

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sources** | **Find a source** for a sentence you select: passages from your own library that support it, each quote confirmed in Zetaris, ready to **Insert** as a citation |
| **Check** | **Check my draft** |
| **Versions** | What changed since a version, and **Restore this version** |
| **Record** | Every check, source search and citation made in this note, with its versions, as Word, PDF or Markdown |

A citation in a note reads like `Nishida and Walker, 2007, p. 4` and opens its source.

## Check my draft

**Check my draft** finds the factual claims in what you wrote (up to 20, from the first 30,000
characters) and looks for each in your sources, not your notes. Each comes back as **Supported**,
**Partly supported**, **Contradicted** or **Not found**, with the passage that decides it and its
quote checked in Zetaris. Claims are underlined in the draft; from each you can **Cite this**,
**Open** the passage, or **Find a source**.

## Cite in any style, export

APA 7th, MLA 9th, Chicago (author-date and notes), Harvard, IEEE, Vancouver, Nature and AMA are
built in, and **Search 2,000+ citation styles** reaches the rest. **Export** writes a Word file, or
(kept accounts) a Google Doc, with your citations and the reference list in the style you choose.

## Publish

**publish ↗** puts a note on your page at `konkyo.xyz/a/<link>`. It needs a kept account with a
handle, and a check of the saved text: the sheet shows **what the check found** (*S of T claims
supported · N partly · N not found · N contradicted*) and the article shows it as it is. Each quote
from your own sources is checked in Zetaris again as it publishes. Page images of the cited
sources appear for openly licensed ones, or for all when you choose **show cited pages of every
source**. Three publishes a day.

The article page lets readers **listen**, **save these sources** into a space of their own, share it,
or report it; a kept account can like it. Your articles, and the public spaces you choose to show,
sit on your page at `konkyo.xyz/u/<handle>`, where people can follow you.
