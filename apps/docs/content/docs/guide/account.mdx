## Guest or kept

| | Guest | Kept account |
|---|---|---|
| Sign in | **enter →** with a name and a look | Google, or a six-digit code by email |
| Where it lives | This browser | Every device |
| Keep it | **keep this on my other devices** moves spaces, sources, memory and practice into an account | |
| Connectors, API keys, your page and handle, follows, publishing and likes, Google Docs export, listing a space on your page, email invites, the weekly digest | | Yes |
| Public links, invite links, joining a space | Yes | Yes |
| Sign out | Deletes the guest and its library | A plain sign out |

## Settings

| Section | What you set |
|---|---|
| **you** | Your name, look or photo; for a kept account, your page (`konkyo.xyz/u/<handle>`) and bio |
| **sounds & voice** | Interface sounds, read answers aloud, Konkyo's voice, study sounds |
| **connectors** | Gmail, Google Drive and Notion: connect, import, watch a Gmail search, disconnect. Konkyo only reads |
| **citations** | Your default citation style (APA unless you change it) |
| **deep search** | **Import sources without asking** |
| **notifications** | Push on this device, the daily review reminder and its hour, the weekly digest |
| **study** | New questions a day (5 to 50, 15 by default) |
| **API keys** | Keys for scripts calling `POST /api/v1/ask` ([CLI and API](/docs/build/cli-and-api)) |
| **usage** | What you've used of today's allowances |
| **sign out & delete** | **Delete account** asks you to type *delete*, then removes everything |

## Daily allowances

Konkyo is free. These count the last 24 hours:

| Allowance | Guest | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| Questions (chat, deep search and @konkyo) | 60 | 300 |
| Deep searches | 3 | 20 |
| Practice sets | 6 | 40 |
| Audio briefs | 1 | 5 |
| Files added | 40 | 300 |
| Voice sessions | 12 | 80 |
| Read aloud (new characters) | 1,500 | 8,000 |
| Publishes | | 3 |

## People

**People** lists your friends, who you follow and who follows you, with **invite a friend** and
**copy my page link**. A kept account with a handle has a public page at `konkyo.xyz/u/<handle>`:
your name, bio, followers, published articles and the public spaces you chose to show.

Deleted things wait in **Trash** for 30 days, then go for good.
