| Partner | Its job in Konkyo | Without it |
|---|---|---|
| [**Zetaris**](/docs/partners/zetaris) | The evidence store: every passage, its own reading of every PDF, and the SQL that checks every quote; topic watches as tables over OpenAlex | Nothing can be marked verified |
| [**Meterless**](/docs/partners/meterless) | H-MEM keeps your corrections; the World Model keeps who said what; Markovian screens deep searches in bounded steps | No memory of your corrections, no record of where sources stand |
| [**NVIDIA Nemotron**](/docs/partners/nvidia) | Plans answers, embeds every passage and question, screens deep-search finds, guards what's public, and stands in for every OpenAI agent role | No search by meaning (every vector is Nemotron's) |
| [**ElevenLabs**](/docs/partners/more#elevenlabs) | Transcribes recordings to the second, records audio briefs, reads answers aloud, hears the orb | No lectures, briefs or voice |
| [**Composio**](/docs/partners/more#composio) | Gmail, Google Drive and Notion imports | Upload by hand |
| [**OpenAlex**](/docs/partners/more#openalex) | Open papers for deep search and watches, and citation records | No open papers in deep search or watches |

Each also has a page on konkyo.xyz that follows one action into the code, with an example (live
for Zetaris and OpenAlex): [made with Zetaris](https://konkyo.xyz/made-with/zetaris),
[Meterless](https://konkyo.xyz/made-with/meterless),
