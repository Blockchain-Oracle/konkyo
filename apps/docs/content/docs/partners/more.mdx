## ElevenLabs

- **Recordings, lectures and YouTube without captions** are transcribed with Scribe (`scribe_v2`),
  word timings and speakers kept, so a citation plays the moment it quotes.
  (`packages/ingest/src/transcript.ts`)
- **Audio briefs**: two voices talk a space through in one Text-to-Dialogue request (`eleven_v3`);
  every line's quote was checked first. (`packages/ingest/src/dialogue.ts`)
- **The orb** hears you through Scribe realtime (`scribe_v2_realtime`), and **▶ listen** reads
  answers with `eleven_flash_v2_5`, word-synced. (`apps/web/lib/voice-server.ts`)
- **Study sounds** (right, try again, hint, round done, day seven) were made once with its sound
  effects model and ship as small files.

## Composio

Gmail, Google Drive and Notion connect through Composio: each person links their own account, and
every tool call is pinned to a measured toolkit version. Konkyo only reads. Attachments, documents
and pages import through the same pipeline as an upload, and Drive and Notion items are re-read
every 15 minutes when they change. (`packages/connectors/src/index.ts`)

## OpenAlex

- **Deep search** looks up open-access papers on OpenAlex when your library runs out.
  (`packages/agents/src/scout.ts`)
- **Citation records**: a DOI, arXiv id or title is matched on OpenAlex for authors and venue.
  (`packages/ingest/src/metadata.ts`)
- **Topic watches** are Zetaris `REST` tables over the OpenAlex API. [Zetaris](/docs/partners/zetaris#topic-watches)

## OpenAI

`gpt-6-luna` is the Writer, Verifier, Analyst, Tutor and Grader, and the fallback for Nemotron's
roles. `gpt-5-nano` runs deep search's web search, with Wikipedia's own search when it fails.
