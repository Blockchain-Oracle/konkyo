## Step by step

1. **Planner.** Reads the corrections you've given Konkyo before (its H-MEM memory), rewrites
   the question so it stands alone, plans one to three searches, and decides whether the question
   weighs several sources against each other.
2. **Retriever.** Searches your passages two ways for each query, by meaning (pgvector) and by
   words (Postgres full text), and fuses the lists. When even the best match is weak, the
   **Planner replans** from what came back (broader terms, synonyms, the words the closest
   passages use) and the Retriever searches again, fusing both rounds. If nothing is close enough
   after that, the answer is "This isn't in your library yet.", never a guess: it lists every search it tried
   and offers **Find sources**, which sends the Scout to open papers and the web.
3. **Zetaris pre-check.** While the Writer writes, confirms the documents behind those passages are in
   the evidence store.
4. **Writer.** Writes one finding per sentence, and is told to name each source by its authors. Each sentence
   carries a citation, and each citation an exact quote copied from its passage. You see the draft
   as it's written, its citations marked as being checked; the checked answer replaces it.
5. **Verifier.** Checks each quote in code (normalised words, verbatim), then checks all of them
   in **one** Zetaris statement against Zetaris's own reading of the source PDF, a second
   extraction independent of Konkyo's. At the same time a model judges whether each sentence's
   quotes support it. Rejected sentences go back to the Writer with reasons, at most twice; whatever still fails
   is dropped. A later round judges only the rewritten sentences: one already approved, with the
   same words and quotes, keeps its verdict, and a quote Zetaris found isn't looked up again.
6. **Answer.** Only what passed. The receipt shows the steps, sources, memories used and the SQL;
   the full trace is written to `logs/<runId>.jsonl`.

When **Compare** is on, or the Planner sees a question that compares sources, and the passages
come from two or more files, the **Analyst** runs beside the Writer: up to six specific claims (it aims for three to six),
each source's stance (agrees, disagrees, partly) with a quote, every quote checked in Zetaris, and
the result recorded in your World Model (and the space's, when you asked in one space).

## The check, as it runs

```sql
SELECT q.qid
FROM VALUES
    ('1', '<document>', '<passage>', 'spindlesarecharacteristicwaxingandwaningoscillations…'),
    ('2', '<document>', '<passage>', 'howevertopographicalsleepspindleanalysisrevealed…')
  AS q(qid, doc, pid, qkey)
JOIN konkyo.konkyo_pdf_text t ON t.doc_id = q.doc
WHERE instr(t.p, q.qkey) > 0
```

A quote counts only if its normalised words appear in Zetaris's text of the document. Recordings
and notes have no PDF, so their quotes are checked against their passages as published to
Zetaris. A Compare answer's receipt shows two statements: one for the answer's quotes, one for the
table's.
