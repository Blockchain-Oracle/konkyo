Konkyo's answers come from agents that disagree with each other on purpose: the Writer drafts,
the Verifier checks its quotes in Zetaris and its claims against the passages, and anything that
doesn't hold goes back. The same team makes practice, checks drafts, writes briefs and runs deep
searches.

## Who does what

| Role | Its job | Primary model | Fallback | Reserve |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Planner** | Rewrites the question to stand alone, plans 1–3 searches, flags a comparison; replans when results are thin | Nemotron 3 Ultra | `gpt-6-luna` | Nemotron 3.5 Lightning |
| **Retriever** | Searches your passages by meaning (Nemotron 3.5 Embed vectors in pgvector) and by words (Postgres full text), fused by rank | Nemotron 3.5 Embed | | |
| **Writer** | The cited draft, one finding per sentence; rewrites; brief scripts; lecture notes; claims in a draft | `gpt-6-luna` | Nemotron 3 Ultra | Nemotron 3.5 Super VL |
| **Verifier** | Judges whether quotes support each sentence; checks practice answer keys, brief lines and draft claims | `gpt-6-luna` | Nemotron 3 Ultra | Nemotron 3.5 Lightning |
| **Analyst** | Claims × sources with stances; places papers a watch finds | `gpt-6-luna` | Nemotron 3 Ultra | Nemotron 3.5 Super VL |
| **Scout** | Plans a deep search | Nemotron 3.5 Lightning | `gpt-6-luna` | Nemotron 3 Ultra |
| **Screener** | Screens what deep search found, in Markovian steps | Nemotron 3 Ultra | `gpt-6-luna` | Nemotron 3.5 Lightning |
| **Tutor** | Drafts practice questions | `gpt-6-luna` | Nemotron 3 Ultra | Nemotron 3.5 Super VL |
| **Grader** | Judges a typed answer the code check couldn't settle | `gpt-6-luna` | Nemotron 3 Ultra | Nemotron 3.5 Lightning |
| **Voice intent** | Reads a spoken ask no rule placed | Nemotron 3.5 Lightning | `gpt-6-luna` | Nemotron 3 Ultra |

Outside the roles: Nemotron 3.5 Embed makes every vector, Nemotron 3.5 Lightning summarises each
file, Nemotron 3.5 Content Safety checks public questions, profiles and articles, and deep search's
web search is OpenAI's `gpt-5-nano` with Wikipedia when it fails. These have no model fallback.

## When a model doesn't answer

Each role tries its models in turn, the ones answering first. A call moves on after an error, a
reply it can't use, or a stall: no answer within 45 seconds, or no first word of a stream within
25 (12 seconds for the Planner on Nemotron and for every Scout model, 8 for voice intent, 90 for
the Tutor). On NVIDIA's free tier
a slow call is also asked again, and the first answer wins. A model that answers "busy" goes to the
back of the line for a minute; an account out of credit, for ten. Every switch in a traced run is
written to the trace as `escalated_to_fallback`.
