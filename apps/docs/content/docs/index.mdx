Konkyo (根拠, "grounds" or "evidence") reads your papers, lecture recordings, slides and notes.
Ask it a question and a team of agents finds the passages that answer it, writes an answer
where every sentence cites a passage, checks every quote word for word against the source, and
sends back anything it can't support. When your sources disagree, it shows you where.

## Why it checks

Citations make an answer look trustworthy whether or not anyone opens them: in one study, only 23
of 319 knowledge workers checked the sources an AI gave them
([Lee et al., CHI 2025](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713778)). So Konkyo opens every citation
for you before you see the answer, and one click on a citation opens the source at the passage,
highlighted.

## What you can do with it

- **Ask** across your whole library, a space, or chosen files, by typing or out loud to the orb.
  Answers carry their receipt: the steps, the passages, and the SQL that checked the quotes. When
  the first search comes back thin, the Planner tries again more broadly before saying "not in
  your library".
- **Compare** sources: turn on **Compare** and the agree/disagree matrix lines up claims against
  sources, each stance quoted. What it found stays on the space's **knows** page.
- **Find sources** when your library runs out: deep search reads open-access papers and web pages.
- **Bring things in** from files, folders, links (an arXiv or journal link reads the paper),
  YouTube, Gmail, Drive and Notion, a lecture recorded in the browser, or mail forwarded to the
  space's own address.
- **Study**: quizzes from your own material, with answer keys checked against their passages and
  spaced repetition; play a **live round** with friends; listen to a two-voice **audio brief**.
- **Agents** watch for new papers, quiz you every Monday, or remake a space's brief when it
  changes; every run reports to your Inbox. Each paper a watch finds says whether it agrees with,
  disagrees with, or is new to your sources, quoting the passage it's compared with.
- **Write**: cite as you write, check a draft against your sources, and publish it as an article
  whose every claim was checked.
- **Share** a space with people who read and add with you, live, or by a public link anyone can
  ask. See [Spaces, sharing and email](/spaces-and-sharing).
