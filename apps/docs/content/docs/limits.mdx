---
title: Limits
description: What Konkyo doesn't do, or doesn't do well yet.
---

- **Two model providers, one fallback each.** OpenAI writes and judges; when it's down or out of
  credit, Nemotron 3 Ultra takes over, slower on NVIDIA's free tier (about 40 requests a minute
  for each model, split between the web app and the worker). Sample mode needs no key at all.
- **Live use needs Zetaris.** Nothing is marked verified without it, by design.
- **"Supported" means the cited passage states it**, not that the claim is true in the world. A
  wrong source, quoted correctly, still passes.
- **Sample mode replays recorded questions only.** Any other question is refused, not invented.
- **Fair-use caps**, no paywall: 300 answers, 20 deep searches and 300 files a day per account;
  guests get less (60, 3, 40). Settings → Usage shows them.
- **Connectors** use Composio-managed Google auth, whose consent screen asks for wider Gmail scopes
  than Konkyo uses (it only reads). A read-only setup needs your own Google OAuth client.
- **Scanned PDFs** are read with OCRmyPDF; handwriting and complex tables can come out imperfect.
- **Deep search** reads only open-access papers it can download; paywalled publishers are skipped.
- **Email** goes through Resend's free plan: 100 emails a day, sent and received together. So
  sign-in codes stop at 50 a day (8 per network address), invites by email at 30 (10 per
  person), and Google sign-in is always there when codes run out.
